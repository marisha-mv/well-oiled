FIX EMAIL DELIVERABILITY (CRITICAL ISSUE)

⚠️ URGENT: Your show-up reminder emails are going to SPAM. This is killing your attendance rate.

The Problem:

  • Reminder emails not reaching inbox
  • Registrants don't see reminders → don't attend
  • Could be 30-50% of your "low show-up rate" problem

Why Emails Go To Spam:

COMMON DELIVERABILITY ISSUES: 1. Domain/IP Reputation - New sending domain without warm-up - High spam complaint rate - Blacklisted IP address 2. Authentication Issues - Missing SPF record - Missing DKIM signature - Missing DMARC policy 3. Content Triggers - Too many links - Spam trigger words ("FREE", "ACT NOW", "LIMITED TIME") - High image-to-text ratio - ALL CAPS in subject lines 4. Engagement Issues - Low open rates historically - High unsubscribe rates - Many invalid email addresses

Solutions To Implement IMMEDIATELY:

STEP 1: Technical Authentication (Priority #1)

  • Set up SPF record: Authorizes your email server to send from your domain
  • Set up DKIM: Cryptographically signs your emails
  • Set up DMARC: Tells email providers how to handle unauthenticated emails
  • Tools: Use MXToolbox.com to check current authentication status

STEP 2: Use A Dedicated Sending Domain

BEST PRACTICE: Main domain: welloiledoperations.com Sending domain: mail.welloiledoperations.com OR events.welloiledoperations.com Why? - Protects main domain reputation - Easier to warm up new sending domain - Can isolate issues to secondary domain FROM addresses should be: [email protected] (warm-up & build trust)

STEP 3: Clean Subject Lines

❌ SPAM TRIGGERS:

"🚨 FREE WORKSHOP STARTING NOW!!!"
"LAST CHANCE - CLICK HERE NOW"
"Re: Your Application [Automated]"
"ACT NOW - LIMITED TIME OFFER"

Issues: Emojis, ALL CAPS, spam words, fake "Re:", urgency

✓ DELIVERABLE:

"Workshop starts in 1 hour"
"Your workshop reminder"
"See you at 11:30am today"
"Quick reminder for today's session"

Better: Natural language, no tricks, clear purpose
